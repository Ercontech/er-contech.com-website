# Er-Con Website Review Checklist

This is the living review checklist for reaching `v.Base`. It focuses first on experience, structure and messaging approach, then on detailed wording and execution.

## 1. Audience and purpose

- [ ] Confirm the primary reader is an asset owner or company trying to solve an erosion, stabilization or asset-protection problem.
- [ ] Confirm the Site helps the reader recognize their situation without attempting to engineer the solution in the browser.
- [ ] Confirm Er-Con is positioned as the problem-solving partner, while Armorform remains a separate product and distribution swimlane.
- [ ] Confirm every page has one clear job and supports the reader's next decision.

## 2. Site structure and navigation

- [ ] Confirm the primary navigation order: Home, Solutions, Projects, Contact, Careers, About.
- [ ] Confirm readers can orient themselves and return to another major page from every page.
- [ ] Confirm solution detail pages provide a clear route back to Solutions.
- [ ] Confirm job detail pages provide a clear route back to Careers.
- [ ] Confirm mobile navigation is complete, legible and easy to close.
- [ ] Confirm Projects navigation lands at the intended project content.

## 3. First-screen hierarchy

- [ ] Review every page at an average desktop monitor size and normal browser scaling.
- [ ] Confirm the first screen reveals the page's primary subject rather than delaying it behind repeated framing.
- [ ] Confirm hero sections are no taller than their narrative purpose requires.
- [ ] Remove homepage messages repeated on interior pages unless the repetition provides useful orientation.
- [ ] Confirm the most important content begins within one reasonable scroll.

## 4. Messaging architecture

- [ ] Confirm the core promise centers on erosion mitigation, prevention, bank stabilization and protecting critical assets.
- [ ] Use "Er-Con Technologies" for formal identification, including page titles, metadata, the first company introduction on Home and About, job-description business-unit references, equal-employment statements, email subject lines and the legal footer.
- [ ] Use "Er-Con" for headlines, normal narrative after the company is established, calls to action, solution and project descriptions and other conversational references.
- [ ] Treat the logo as establishing the full company name on secondary pages rather than forcing "Er-Con Technologies" into every hero.
- [ ] Confirm "Experience. Innovation. Results." is used with appropriate prominence and restraint.
- [ ] Confirm more than 50 years, 3,000+ global projects and 35+ U.S. states are presented consistently.
- [ ] Use global or international reach without unnecessarily limiting the reader's imagination to a short country list.
- [ ] Treat safety as an operating expectation, not a generic differentiating claim.
- [ ] Prefer dedicated in-house teams over vague references to internal capabilities.
- [ ] Confirm claims distinguish real Er-Con capabilities from comparisons, alternatives and unsupported offerings.

## 5. Solutions experience

- [ ] Confirm the page shows the core solutions before the methodology explanation.
- [ ] Confirm the six core solution cards are distinct, understandable and linked to the correct detail pages.
- [ ] Confirm product and system names, trademarks and capitalization are accurate.
- [ ] Confirm the Solution Framework demonstrates a deliberate, engineering-led methodology rather than an arbitrary product choice.
- [ ] Confirm the framework shows root-cause evaluation, comparison of available systems and customer review before execution.
- [ ] Confirm the framework communicates that Er-Con can tailor or combine multiple products based on site conditions.
- [ ] Confirm fabrication language accurately reflects site-specific factory fabrication rather than fabrication at the project site.
- [ ] Confirm additional capabilities are valid Er-Con offerings and do not dilute the core solutions.
- [ ] Confirm no precast articulating block mat offering is presented as an Er-Con service or product.

## 6. Projects and proof

- [x] Create a dedicated Projects page and route site-wide Projects navigation to it.
- [x] Keep a concise selected-project preview on Home with a clear path to the full Projects page.
- [x] Distinguish Solutions as what Er-Con can configure from Projects as where those solutions have been applied.
- [ ] Confirm project examples help readers see situations similar to their own.
- [ ] Confirm project imagery, titles and categories match the actual work shown.
- [ ] Confirm project selectors work with mouse, keyboard and touch.
- [ ] Determine whether additional project context or results are needed without turning the page into technical case studies.
- [ ] Confirm all project counts and geographic claims are supportable.
- [ ] Determine where landowners should be represented as project stakeholders or decision influencers, especially when property damage prompts pipeline owners to act.

## 6A. Local impact and site stewardship

- [x] Add a concise Home-page message explaining that Er-Con brings its dedicated teams, systems and project leadership while working with local contractors, equipment providers, material suppliers and additional labor where practical.
- [x] Communicate that local sourcing supports the communities where Er-Con works without suggesting that ownership of the project is outsourced.
- [x] Communicate Er-Con's commitment to minimizing its footprint and leaving each site better than it was found.
- [ ] Support the stewardship message with customer recognition, project examples or testimonials when approved evidence is available.
- [ ] Consider a shorter supporting version on the About page after the primary Home-page treatment is established.

## 7. Calls to action and contact

- [ ] Confirm repeated calls to action are intentionally placed and not duplicative within the same page.
- [ ] Prefer a better first conversation over an online diagnosis.
- [ ] Ask for useful field context: current conditions, concerns, relevant assets, changes observed and available drawings or photos.
- [ ] Confirm the Contact form excludes photo upload until a supported workflow is selected.
- [ ] Confirm form submissions preserve and deliver the visitor's entered text.
- [ ] Confirm all phone numbers, email addresses and mailto links are accurate.
- [ ] Confirm each page ends with an appropriate next step.

## 8. About and leadership

- [ ] Confirm the origin story is accurate, distinctive and appropriately concise.
- [ ] Confirm the leadership titles are current.
- [ ] Replace illustrated portraits with approved photographs when available.
- [ ] Add approved LinkedIn links rather than rebuilding long biography sections.
- [ ] Confirm leadership links have clear labels and appropriate external-link behavior.

## 9. Careers

- [x] Provide an open-door resume path for exceptional candidates who do not see a matching posted role.
- [ ] Confirm the Careers overview reflects the type of organization and field work candidates are joining.
- [ ] Confirm every open position appears on the Careers page and links to the correct detail page.
- [ ] Confirm role titles are market-appropriate, including Field Construction Technician rather than Laborer.
- [ ] Confirm each job detail page accurately reflects its approved job description.
- [ ] Confirm department, location, reporting relationship and employment details are consistent.
- [ ] Confirm Apply buttons open the intended email workflow with a useful subject line.
- [ ] Confirm resume wording is presented without accent marks.
- [ ] Remove or archive closed positions promptly.

- [ ] Home: review every headline, paragraph, label, statistic, project caption, button and footer line.
- [ ] Solutions: review every headline, framework step, solution-card description, link label and supporting capability.
- [ ] Palisade: review every word and validate all technical and application claims.
- [ ] Palisade field sequence: confirm project number, location, dates, customer-use permission and that the three photographs document the same reach.
- [ ] Flexible Concrete Revetment: review every word and validate all technical and application claims.
- [ ] FCR comparison sets: confirm the project numbers, locations, dates, customer-use permissions and pairing of each before-and-after photograph.
- [ ] Ercoform: review every word and validate all technical and application claims.
- [ ] Ercoform comparison sets: confirm the project numbers, locations, dates, customer-use permissions and pairing of each field-condition and completed-application photograph.
- [ ] Ercoform comparison framing: confirm the "channel and crossing protection" and "localized channel stabilization" interpretations with the technical team.
- [ ] Ercoform drawing: confirm that the standard mat detail, dimensions, terminology and grout-tube configuration remain current and approved for public use.
- [ ] Eco-Flex: review every word and validate all technical and application claims.
- [ ] Eco-Flex imagery: obtain and verify a paired field-condition and completed-application project sequence, including project number, location, date and customer-use permission.
- [ ] Eco-Flex drawing: confirm the recovered mat detail and typical cross section remain technically current and appropriate for public use.
- [ ] System 21: review every word and validate all technical and application claims.
- [ ] System 21: replace the proof-section placeholders with a verified Field Condition / Completed Application project pair.
- [ ] System 21: confirm that the legacy second image is a representative completed System 21 application before final public launch.
- [ ] Pipeline Supports: review every word and validate all technical and application claims.
- [ ] Pipeline Supports: confirm that the selected Field Condition and Completed Application photographs are the same project and approved for public use.
- [ ] Pipeline Supports: validate the soil-analysis, piling-suitability and required-depth language with the technical team.
- [ ] Pipeline Supports schematic: confirm that the clamp, insulator, cross-beam, barrel-sleeve and pipe-pile labels still represent the current product configuration.
- [ ] Pipeline Supports schematic: specifically confirm or remove the legacy note stating pipe-pile depth may extend to 25 feet as required.
- [ ] About: review every word, date, proof point, leadership name and title.
- [ ] Contact: review every instruction, field label, required indicator, button and contact detail.
- [ ] Careers: review every headline, introduction, opening summary, label and button.
- [ ] Project Manager: compare every displayed word with the approved job description.
- [ ] Field Construction Technician: compare every displayed word with the approved job description.
- [ ] Civil Designer: compare every displayed word with the approved job description.
- [ ] Regional Account Manager: compare every displayed word with the approved job description.
- [ ] Review navigation, footer, browser titles, metadata descriptions, image alternative text and form-generated email text across all pages.
- [ ] Confirm no visible copy contains an em dash.
- [ ] Confirm punctuation, capitalization, trademark symbols, hyphenation and terminology are consistent site-wide.

## 11. Visual system and imagery

- [ ] Replace recovered web-resolution thumbnails with approved original-resolution project photography where available.
- [ ] Confirm the project number, product assignment, location and permission status for every recovered legacy image.
- [ ] Build each product page around a curated proof sequence: field condition, root cause, engineered drawing, installation and performance today.
- [ ] Confirm public drawings omit customer identifiers, precise coordinates and sensitive infrastructure details.
- [ ] Confirm Articulating Block Mat language describes Er-Con's custom-fabricated form grouted in place and does not imply a precast product.
- [ ] Confirm the logo treatment is consistent and legible at desktop and mobile sizes.
- [ ] Replace generic or inaccurate engineering imagery with approved Er-Con imagery where needed.
- [ ] Confirm every image supports the nearby message and is not merely decorative filler.
- [ ] Confirm crops remain useful at common desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
- [ ] Confirm card heights, section transitions, borders and spacing feel intentional.
- [ ] Confirm there are no overlaps, clipped elements or sections bleeding into adjacent backgrounds.

## 12. Responsive, accessibility and interaction

- [ ] Review all pages on desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
- [ ] Review at 200% browser zoom for readable text and usable controls.
- [ ] Confirm keyboard focus is visible and navigation order is logical.
- [ ] Confirm headings follow a sensible hierarchy.
- [ ] Confirm images have useful alternative text when they carry meaning.
- [ ] Confirm color contrast is sufficient for body text, links and buttons.
- [ ] Confirm buttons and links clearly describe their destination or action.
- [ ] Confirm there is no unintended horizontal scrolling.

## 13. Final path to v.Base

- [ ] Complete the full page-by-page word review.
- [ ] Resolve all factual and technical-content questions with the appropriate Er-Con owner.
- [ ] Replace remaining placeholders and temporary imagery.
- [ ] Confirm all links, forms and interactive elements.
- [ ] Complete desktop and mobile visual review.
- [ ] Complete metadata, accessibility and consistency review.
- [ ] Approve the complete site as `v.Base` before launch preparation.
